    About me

    I am Jingfei Wang (王敬飞), a Ph.D. student in Probability and Statistics at the School of Mathematical Sciences, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), supervised by Prof. Lijun Bo.

    From January 2024 to December 2025, I was a research assistant in the Department of Applied Mathematics at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, working with Prof. Xiang Yu.

    My research lies at the interface of stochastic control, mean field theory and reinforcement learning. For further information, please see my CV. (updated 2026-11)

    Research Interests

    • Stochastic Control and Optimization
    • Mean Field Games and Mean Field Control
    • Reinforcement Learning in Continuous Time
    • Mathematical Finance
